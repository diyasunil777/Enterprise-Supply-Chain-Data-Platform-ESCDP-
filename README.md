# Enterprise-Supply-Chain-Data-Platform-ESCDP-
 Enterprise Supply Chain Data Platform (ESCDP): Building a Unified Supply  Chain &amp; Logistics Analytics Platform

Business Background 
ABC Global Manufacturing Ltd. operates a global supply chain network comprising multiple 
manufacturing plants, distribution centers, warehouses, logistics partners, and retail outlets 
across different regions. Every day, thousands of business transactions are generated from 
Supply Chain Management (SCM), Warehouse Management System (WMS), 
Transportation Management System (TMS), Inventory Management, Enterprise 
Resource Planning (ERP), Procurement, Vendor Management, Logistics Tracking, and 
Order Fulfillment Systems. As these systems operate independently, the organization faces 
challenges in monitoring inventory levels, tracking shipments, optimizing warehouse 
operations, reducing delivery delays, improving supplier collaboration, and gaining end-to-end 
visibility across the supply chain.
